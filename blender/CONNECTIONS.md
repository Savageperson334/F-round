# What connected

Generated by `make_smoothie.py` from a real run. Nothing here is hand-picked.

- **6158** functions were harvested and **2901** of them ran on something when tried.
- In the blend: **1790** Python functions (**604** of them numpy code, **82** from notebooks) and **1038** JavaScript functions.
- **151371** pairs of functions from different repositories were tried by feeding one's real output to the other (every pair that fits by name, and a random sample of those that fit only by shape).
- **93354** pairs really work (61.7%). The rest raised an error, returned nothing, or ignored what they were given.
- **1146** of the working pairs fit by name: what the first says it produces is what the second says it takes. The others fit only by shape (a number is a number).
- The connected functions come from **409** repositories on **53** shelves, and join **1241** different pairs of shelves.
